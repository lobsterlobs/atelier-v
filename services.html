What's included in each package

Essential — €499

Logo Design

Two independent logo concepts developed based on your questionnaire. You choose one concept and receive up to two revisions. The final logo is delivered in PNG format for digital use and SVG format for print and scaling without loss of quality.

Color Palette & Typography

Three to four primary brand colors and two fonts — one for headings and one for body text. Selected based on your brand and industry. Applied consistently across all digital materials.

Digital Business Card

A personal web page accessible via link and QR code. Contains your contact information, phone, email, social media links and a button to save the contact directly to any phone. Branded in your logo style.

Website

A one-page website with navigation including sections: home, about us, services and contact. Fully responsive — works on mobile, tablet and desktop. Built in HTML/CSS — fast, secure and requires no admin panel.

Technical Setup

Domain registration and connection, SSL certificate and website publication. You handle nothing technical.

Hosting

24 months of free hosting. Your website is online and accessible 24/7.

Not included: texts and photos provided by client, domains with non-standard market value, revisions beyond included, content translation.

Delivery: 7 working days from receipt of completed questionnaire and confirmed payment.

Full Brand — €799

Includes everything in Essential, plus:

Multi-page Website

Separate pages for each section — home, about, services, gallery, contact. Up to five pages included. Each page is standalone with full content and navigation.

Branded Email Signature

HTML signature compatible with Gmail and Outlook. Includes logo, contact information and link to your website. Every email you send looks professional and consistent.

Multilingual Support

Up to five languages of your choice. The website includes automatic language switching. Ideal for businesses with international clients or tourists.

Revisions

Two rounds of revisions included. One round represents one list of changes submitted by the client and applied by Atelier V within 48 hours.

Not included: more than five pages, online store or payment system, blog system, domains with non-standard market value.

Delivery: 7 working days.

Total Brand — From €2,500

Complete digital presence for corporate clients and businesses with complex needs. Includes everything in Full Brand, plus digital strategy, social media management, marketing and advertising, and corporate identity. Terms and scope are agreed individually. Contact us for a free consultation.

Terms of Service

Domain

Every package includes one domain provided by Atelier V. The domain is selected jointly with the client based on the business name and activity. Atelier V covers the registration costs for the first year. The client receives full ownership of the domain after final payment. After the first year the domain is renewed at the client's expense. Atelier V notifies the client 30 days before expiry and assists with renewal. If the client already owns a domain — Atelier V connects it to the new website free of charge.

Hosting

Every package includes 24 months of hosting at no additional charge. The website is accessible 24/7. After the included period the client may continue with the current hosting or choose an alternative. Atelier V assists with the transition.

Payment

30% of the total amount is paid before work begins. The remaining 70% is paid after review of the finished product and before final publication. In case of delayed payment Atelier V reserves the right to pause work until the obligation is settled.

Revisions

A revision means changes to existing elements — not building a new design. One revision round represents one list of changes submitted by the client and applied by Atelier V within 48 hours. Additional revisions beyond those included are agreed individually.

Intellectual Property

After final payment the client receives full rights over all delivered materials — logo, website, files. Atelier V reserves the right to use completed projects as portfolio references unless the client explicitly requests confidentiality in writing.

Liability

The client guarantees that all provided materials do not infringe any copyright or applicable law. Atelier V is not liable for hosting service interruptions by third parties, data loss due to technical issues, or changes made by the client independently after publication.

Confidentiality
